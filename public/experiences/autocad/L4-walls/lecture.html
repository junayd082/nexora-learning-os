Lesson 4 · Lecture

Building the Walls

A wall has thickness, but an AutoCAD line has none. So every wall is two parallel lines, exactly the wall thickness apart. In class we use 6" exterior walls and 4" interior walls.

90-minute blockAutoCAD 2026Practice House · 30'-0" × 24'-0"
Essential question

How does a single line become a wall with real thickness?

Students will build the practice house exterior walls with OFFSET, add 4" interior walls, and clean every intersection with TRIM, EXTEND and FILLET.

I can…
  • I can OFFSET the outline 6" inward to make exterior walls.
  • I can place interior walls at exact distances.
  • I can TRIM wall intersections cleanly.
  • I can fix corners with EXTEND or FILLET.

4.1The OFFSET Command

OFFSET (O) makes a parallel copy of a line, polyline or rectangle at an exact distance. It is the main wall-building tool.

Example: OFFSET prompts

Command: O
Specify offset distance or [Through/Erase/Layer] <Through>:
6
Select object to offset or [Exit/Undo] <Exit>:
Click the house rectangle
Specify point on side to offset or [Exit/Multiple/Undo] <Exit>:
Click INSIDE the rectangle
Press Enter or Esc to finish
Try it Yourself »
Note

OFFSET remembers the distance. Next time just press Enter to accept it.

4.2Exterior Walls

  1. Set A-WALL current.
  2. Use your 30'-0" × 24'-0" rectangle from Lesson 2 (outside face of the house).
  3. Type O, enter 6, select the rectangle, click inside.
  4. Check: the inner rectangle is 29'-0" × 23'-0".
Watch out

If you click outside, the house grows to 31'-0" × 25'-0". The rectangle you drew is the true outside size, so the wall must go inward. Undo and click inside.

4.3Interior Walls

The practice house has three interior walls. To place them exactly, turn the inner rectangle into separate lines with EXPLODE (X), then offset those lines.

Interior wallOffset this lineDistanceThen offset again
North–south wall (18'-0" from the west outside face)Inner west face17'-6"4"
East–west wall, west side (14'-0" from the south outside face)Inner south face13'-6"4"
East–west wall, east side (9'-0" from the south outside face)Inner south face8'-6"4"
Note

Why 17'-6"? The wall is 18'-0" from the outside face, and the inner face is already 6" in: 18'-0" − 6" = 17'-6".

4.4TRIM and EXTEND

Offset lines run the full length of the house, so they cross walls they shouldn't. TRIM (TR) removes the extra pieces.

  • In AutoCAD 2026, TRIM starts in Quick mode: every object is a cutting edge automatically.
  • Click each piece you want removed, or drag a line across several pieces at once.
  • Hold Shift while clicking to EXTEND a line instead.
  • EXTEND (EX) lengthens a line until it meets the next object.
Tip

Trim the short pieces of exterior wall between the two faces of an interior wall so the rooms connect. Zoom in close to every intersection.

4.5Clean Corners with FILLET

FILLET (F) joins two lines at a corner. With a radius of 0 it makes a sharp, perfect corner, trimming or extending both lines as needed.

Example: FILLET with radius 0

Command: F
Select first object or [Undo/Polyline/Radius/Trim/Multiple]:
R
Specify fillet radius <0'-0">:
0
Click the first line, then the second line
Try it Yourself »
Tip

Measure with DIST (DI): click two points and AutoCAD reports the exact distance.

Summary

What to remember

  • A wall = two parallel lines the wall thickness apart.
  • Exterior: OFFSET the outline 6" INSIDE (inner size 29' × 23').
  • Interior: EXPLODE, OFFSET exact distances, then 4" for the second face.
  • TRIM the crossings; EXTEND or FILLET 0 to close corners.
